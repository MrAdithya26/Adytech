# Adytech (working name) – tech in Telugu, basic to advanced

The tech-content arm of ABD Innovations: a YouTube channel (planned) and the Tech & AI section of Adithya Info Hub at **[adithyainfohub.com/tech-ai/](https://adithyainfohub.com/tech-ai/)** (adithyainfohub.com/tech/ redirects there). PC, mobile, internet and online safety, and AI, every piece marked Basic, Intermediate or Advanced, for Telugu viewers in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana and a wider Indian audience.

> The public name is not final (planner task tec-01). The Registration Plan of 31 Oct 2026 proposes **Adygeek** ("Adygeek by Adithya Info Hub"). See `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
