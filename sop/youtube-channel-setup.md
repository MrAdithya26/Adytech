# SOP – YouTube channel set-up · Level: Basic

Do this only after decisions D1 (name), D2 (separate channel or AIH series) and D6 (ownership) are made. YouTube moves menus often; check each step on the day.

## 1. Create the channel (owner: proprietor)
1. Sign in to YouTube with the proprietor's Google account (the same name as Udyam, bank and AdSense, per the Registration Plan).
2. Open YouTube settings → **Add or manage your channel(s)** → **Create a channel** → enter the chosen name. This makes a Brand Account channel, which can have several managers.
3. Settings → **Permissions**: invite Adithya and team members as **Manager** or **Editor**. Never share the owner's password.
4. Turn on 2-Step Verification on every account that manages the channel.

## 2. Verify and unlock features
1. Go to **youtube.com/verify** and verify by phone. This unlocks custom thumbnails, videos longer than 15 minutes and live streaming.
2. YouTube Studio → **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility**: check which features are on.

## 4. Upload defaults (Studio → Settings → Upload defaults)
- Title placeholder: `| <Level>`
- Description: the footer block from `templates/youtube-metadata.md`
- Category: Science & Technology · Language: Telugu · Licence: Standard YouTube
- Comments: "Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review"

Create one per Phase 1 series (S1–S5 in `content/series-plan.md`), each with an English + Telugu description.
