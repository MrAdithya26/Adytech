Rules
1. Readable on a phone at the size of a thumbnail: test by shrinking to 25%.
2. No other creator's thumbnail, no copied images, no fake "shock" faces or arrows pointing at nothing.
3. Brand logos only to show what the video is about, never as if the brand made the video.
4. Save the Canva link and the exported PNG in the episode folder (PNG in Drive, not GitHub, if large).
