# YouTube metadata – template

## Title
- Max ~60 characters visible on mobile. Telugu + English mix. Promise the result, not the topic.
- Pattern: `<Result> | <Telugu hook> | <Level>` — e.g. `Phone Storage Full? 10 GB Free చేయండి | Basic`
- Give Claude's three options and pick one; never clickbait that the video does not deliver.

## Thumbnail text
- Max 4 words, large. Level badge (BASIC / INTERMEDIATE / ADVANCED) in the same corner every time.
- See `thumbnail-brief.md`.

## Tags (YouTube uses them lightly; 8–12 is enough)
`<topic in English>, <topic in Telugu>, <tool name>, telugu tech, tech in telugu, <level> tutorial, <pillar>, adithya info hub`

## Upload settings
- Category: Science & Technology · Video language: Telugu · Caption language: Telugu / English
- Altered or synthetic content: tick "Yes" if realistic AI-generated people, places or events are shown
- Paid promotion box: tick if a brand paid or gave the product
- Made for kids: No
- End screen: next episode in series + subscribe. Cards: the post link (when the channel is eligible) or a related video
