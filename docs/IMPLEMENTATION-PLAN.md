## 1. What Adytech is

Adytech is the tech-content arm of ABD Innovations. It teaches technology in Telugu, from very basic to advanced, on two connected platforms:

| Platform | Address | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website section | adithyainfohub.com/tech-ai/ (adithyainfohub.com/tech/ already redirects here) | Written guides in English and Telugu; search traffic; every video gets a post |
| YouTube | Channel or series to be decided (Decision D2) | Long videos (8–15 min) and Shorts; the main growth engine |

**Four content pillars**

| Pillar | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| 1. PC | Windows tips, shortcuts, free software, speed-ups, backups, bootable USB |
| 2. Mobile | Android tips, settings, useful apps, phone-to-PC transfer, storage clean-up |
| 3. Internet and safety | Browsers, Google tricks, UPI and OTP scams, passwords, 2-step verification |
| 4. AI | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and other tools in Telugu; agentic AI; GitHub repos; new launches |

**Three levels** on every piece: **Basic** (first-time users), **Intermediate** (comfortable users who want more), **Advanced** (terminal, GitHub, local AI, automation).

**Audience:** Telugu speakers in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana first (students, job aspirants, small-business owners, parents, office staff), then a wider Indian audience through English posts.

**What Adytech is not:** the paid tech-services business (starter websites, chatbots) at tech.agservices.in. That stays in `ABD-Innovations/projects/ADYTECH.md`. Adytech content sends leads to it (see §10).

## 6. The weekly production system (Phase 1 onward)

One long video a week is the anchor. Everything else is cut from it.

```
Topic bank ──► Script (Claude) ──► Fact-check (Adithya on a real device)
     ──► Record ──► Edit ──► Review (checklist) ──► Upload long video (Sat)
     ──► 3 Shorts cut from it (Mon/Wed/Fri)
     ──► English post + Telugu post on AIH, video embedded (Sun)
     ──► Social posts (aih-social-distribution skill) ──► Tracker updated
```

| Day | Work | Who | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunday | 30-min review: last week's numbers, pick next week's topic from the topic bank, ask Claude for the script pack | Adithya + team | 30 min |
| Monday | Claude delivers the script pack: long script, 3 Short scripts, titles, thumbnail text, description, tags, English and Telugu post drafts | Claude | — |
| Mon–Tue | Fact-check on a real PC/phone; note every version number and screen difference | Adithya | 45 min |
| Wednesday | Record screen and voice | Adithya | 1 hr |
| Thu–Fri | Edit (team), thumbnail (team, Canva), review against checklist | Team | 3 hr |
| Saturday 7 pm | Long video goes live; post links in WhatsApp/Telegram | Team | 15 min |
| Sunday | Publish English and Telugu posts with the video embedded | Team | 1 hr |
| Mon/Wed/Fri 7 pm | Shorts | Team | 10 min each |

**Batching (recommended from week 5):** record two long videos on one Wednesday every other week, so one missed week does not break the slot.

**Script pack request** (paste into this Claude project):
```
SCRIPT PACK: <topic from topic bank, ID> | Level: <Basic/Intermediate/Advanced> | Length: <8–12 min>
```
Claude checks current versions, prices and features on the web before writing, and flags anything that may be out of date.

## 8. Formats and quality rules

| Format | Length | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long video | 8–15 min | Hook (0–15 s) → what you'll learn → steps on screen → common mistakes → recap → one call to action |
| Short | 30–60 s, vertical | Result first → 3 steps → "full video" link |
| English post | 900–1,500 words | Summary box, level badge, steps with own screenshots, FAQ, video embed, sources |
| Telugu post | 700–1,200 words | Same structure, mixed Telugu, English for menu names and commands |
| Hands-on guide | as needed | Requirements, exact steps, copy-paste commands, expected result, how to undo |

**Rules on every piece**

1. Level badge (Basic / Intermediate / Advanced) on the thumbnail corner, in the title or description, and in the post.
2. Every version number, price and feature is checked on the day of writing and again on the day of recording. The post says "Checked on <date>".
3. Every tool and GitHub repo is credited with its real name and link. No invented features: if it was not tested on a real device, it is not claimed.
4. Copy-paste commands appear in the description or pinned comment and in the post, in code blocks.
5. Own screenshots and screen recordings only. No copied thumbnails, no other creators' clips.
6. No piracy, cracked software, modded APKs, account hacking or bypassing paywalls. Security topics are defensive only.
7. Paid or affiliate links: disclosed in the video, the description and the post; YouTube's paid-promotion box ticked when a brand pays.
8. Nothing from Adithya's office work or election data appears in any content.
9. Every change to plans or templates is logged in `CHANGELOG.md` and saved in Drive (standing rules).

## 11. YouTube monetisation thresholds (checked 31 Oct 2026)

From YouTube's own help page ([Overview of the expanded YouTube Partner Program](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240)); India is included in the expanded programme.

| Tier | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Expanded (fan funding, Shopping) | 500 subscribers · 3 public uploads in last 90 days · 3,000 public watch hours in last 12 months **or** 3M public Shorts views in last 90 days |
| Ad revenue sharing | 1,000 subscribers · 4,000 public watch hours in last 12 months **or** 10M public Shorts views in last 90 days |

YouTube changes these from time to time. Check the page again before applying.
