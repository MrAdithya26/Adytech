## Series rules

1. Each series has its own thumbnail colour band and the level badge in the same corner.
2. Episode 1 of every series says who it is for and what the viewer will be able to do by the end.
3. Each episode ends by pointing to the next episode in the same series (end screen + card).
4. A series pauses if its last 3 episodes are all in the bottom third of the channel's views; its remaining topics go back into the topic bank.
5. Phase 2 and 3 series start only after the previous phase gate is met.
